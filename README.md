### Project Overview

This project explores sales and inventory data for a fictional toy retail company called Maven Toys, based in Mexico. The goal is to analyze product performance, store performance, and inventory trends in order to support a business expansion plan.
As a BI consultant brought in by the company, my task is to build a Power BI dashboard that reveals actionable insights and helps guide decisions about where to open new stores and how to optimize product distribution.
The dataset includes over 800,000 sales transactions and details about products, stores, and current inventory levels.


### Objectives
- Analyze overall and store-level sales performance
- Identify best-selling and low-performing products
- Examine inventory levels and restocking needs
- Discover regional trends and customer demand patterns
- Propose expansion strategies based on sales and location data

### Tools Used
- Power BI
- Excel
- DAX

### Insights summary 
The Maven Toy Shop Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of the business's financial and sales performance as of August 02, 2025. Key insights include:Financial Overview: Total sales stand at $14.46M, with a total cost of $10.43M, resulting in a total profit of $4.01M.
Sales by Product: Lego bricks lead with 2.4M units sold, followed by Colorbricks (1M), Magic Sand (1M), and Deck of Cards (0.3M).
Profit by Product: Action figures generate the highest profit at $0.35M, followed by Lego bricks ($0.3M), Deck of Cards ($0.25M), and Magic Sand ($0M).
Unit Sales by Location: Downtown accounts for 11.5% of total units sold, Commercial 24%, and Residential 62%, with Airport at 2.5%.
Profit by Category: Action figures lead with $1M, followed by Toys & Games B ($0.5M), and other categories varying between $0.3M and $0.7M.
Profit by Store: Maven Toys Cuidad de Mexico 2 tops with $12K, followed by Maven Toys Cuidad de Mexico 1 ($5K).
Total Profit by Region: Europe shows the highest profit at $300K.
Trend Insight: The Total Profit by Month graph indicates a declining trend from March to October 2024.

The dashboard highlights strong sales in Lego bricks and action figures, with residential areas driving unit sales, while Europe stands out regionally. However, the profit decline suggests potential areas for strategic review.
